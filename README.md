# Nu-investment-thesis
DCF valuation and BUY thesis on Nu Holdings (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia)
## Summary
BUY thesis on Nu Holdings (digital bank and lender across Brazil, Mexico, Colombia). 
DCF value $16.15 vs. market price $14.64 (~10% upside).

## What's inside
- `Nu_Holdings_Thesis.pdf`: full investment memo
- `Nu_DCF_Model.xlsx`: valuation model

## Approach
- Analyzed business model, unit economics and competitive positioning
- Built DCF (revenue growth, margins, capital requirements, terminal value)
- Benchmarked valuation and operating metrics against peers
- Assessed macro, regulatory and execution risks by market

## Key risks
High FX risk. 
No first mover advantage in Mexico and Colombia 
Legal regulations capping credit card and late interest fees (Law 14,690/2023)
Single vendor reliance (MasterCard)
